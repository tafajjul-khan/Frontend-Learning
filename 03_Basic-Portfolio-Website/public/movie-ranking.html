The Best Movies And Series According to Tafajjul

My Top 3 Series And Movies

Series

Stranger Things

A small town where kids start disappearing and strange things begin to happen. A secret government experiment opens a door to another dimension filled with monsters. At its core, the story is about friendship, loyalty, and the consequences of curiosity.

The Playlist

Based on the true story behind Spotify. A tech visionary challenges the music industry, facing legal battles, greedy labels, and internal conflicts. It shows how vision alone isn’t enough—execution, timing, and business politics decide success.

Wednesday

Wednesday Addams joins a strange academy for outcasts and ends up solving a murder mystery. Dark humor, mystery, and self-discovery combine to show that being different is powerful, and pretending to fit in is the real weakness.

Movies

The Social Network

The rise of Facebook and the personal cost paid by its creator. It highlights ambition, betrayal, and how building something massive can destroy relationships along the way.

Inception

A mind-bending thriller where dreams exist inside other dreams. Reality and imagination blur as characters try to plant ideas in the subconscious. The film explores how powerful and dangerous ideas can be.

Whiplash

An intense story of a young drummer and a ruthless mentor. The movie dives into obsession, discipline, and the painful price of greatness, questioning how far someone should go to be the best.
